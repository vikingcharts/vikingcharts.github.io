Building Calculator

  • View total Alfr Marble and Gold costs for every building level.
  • Click to toggle individual buildings, or quickly Select All / Deselect All for any level.
  • Checkboxes with a red border indicate direct dependencies. You can also hover over building names to view their dependencies.
  • Note for Palace: Palace requirements look for dependencies (Wall, Oracle, War Bloc) from the previous level instead of the current level.
  • Totals for each level and the overall Grand Total update instantly as you make changes.
  • Hover over numbers to see full amounts.
